# Exact p-adic denominator laws and exceptional root interpolation in radical renormalization

**Patrick E. Scuracchio — Independent researcher**

**Version 0.9.4 — preliminary research manuscript, preparation stage.** The complete manuscript has not yet undergone independent mathematical review by the author or peer review. No journal acceptance, certified novelty, or publication date is asserted.

## Research

For a radical recurrence of integer root order r ≥ 2, the manuscript establishes exact denominator formulas for the associated renormalized series, isolates an exceptional 3-adic branch, and constructs an integral formal interpolation of its exceptional roots. It also gives exact finite-difference and valuation identities. The Koenigs identification is classical; the comparison with Kallat distinguishes disk-coordinate interpolation from stronger formal integral-coordinate control.
