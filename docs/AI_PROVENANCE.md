# Research provenance and use of generative AI

**Companion research provenance for the v0.9.4 preliminary manuscript.** Author: Patrick E. Scuracchio, independent researcher. Prepared 27 November 2026. Publication is not asserted by this version identifier.

## Origin and research direction

The author conceived the original radical recurrence and its renormalized constants before consulting AI. This arose from an earlier investigation of nested radicals and half-angle constructions of trigonometric functions. The author pursued numerical experiments in the quadratic case with AI assistance, identified the dyadic denominator pattern with OEIS A120738, and directed the generalizations and subsequent proof investigations. The associated combinatorial product interpretation was supplied by AI, not by the author. The precise origin of the earliest complete proof of the dyadic law is not established by the available records; the author recalls that it was probably developed by AI.

The subsequent mathematical investigation relied extensively on generative AI. The author selected problems and research directions, requested alternative proof strategies and audits, curated the resulting materials, and made editorial decisions. The following records distinguish research contribution, proof development, text generation, and verification.

## Systems and model labels

- OpenAI ChatGPT (including Deep Research for earlier literature investigations): mathematical exploration, theorem and proof development, audits, and research synthesis. Certain historical sessions were recorded under the label "Astra"; their precise model identities cannot be established from the available records.
- Anthropic Claude and Claude Code: mathematical explorations, checks, adversarial audits, literature work, and full manuscript drafting. The November drafting and correction records identify Claude Code Opus 5.5.
- OpenAI Codex: November AI referee report, focal comparison with Kallat, corrections and final technical checks, including the v0.9.3 bibliographic revision. The author's reports identify GPT-6.2 Astra (Extra High) for the referee and focal comparison, and GPT-6.2 Sol (Extra High) for the v0.9.2 final check. These are recorded model labels, not independently measured settings.
- The exact model labels of some historical ChatGPT sessions, including the isometry derivation and the initial Kallat note, remain uncertain. No substitute labels are assigned retrospectively.

## Verification and limitations

The research included mathematical reconstructions by different AI systems, exact-arithmetic experiments, bibliographic checking, and local repairs. The AI audits used common project materials and had prior exposure to earlier reports. They were not blind peer review. Intermediate AI-generated claims that did not withstand checking were discarded.

The author has performed personal mathematical checks in the course of the recorded research conversations. The scope of those checks has not been established theorem by theorem. Independent mathematical review by the author of the complete manuscript has not yet been completed. No peer review or journal acceptance is claimed. The author is the sole human author; the AI systems are not authors.

Internal research dates describe the chronology of the private research records, not dates of public dissemination or an established claim of scientific priority. This provenance account records the substantial involvement of AI rather than treating it as mere language editing.
